How it works

No app for guests, no new hardware for you, no integration needed to start.

Your team says yes

Questions hotels ask

Do guests need to install anything?

No. They scan the QR with the phone camera and the hotel's page opens in the browser. No sign-up, no app store.

Do we need to connect our PMS?

Not to start. You set up services, prices and opening hours in the console. A PMS connection can come later.

How do payments work?

The guest's card is held when they order and charged when the service is given. If you can't say yes, the hold is released and the guest pays nothing.

What does my team have to do?

Confirm or decline each order in Telegram, then deliver it. That's it.

How long does it take to start?

Most of the work is listing your services and prices. Once that's done, print the cards and put them in the rooms.
